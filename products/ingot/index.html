这个产品想解决什么

  • 把 HEX / S19 / BIN 当作地址映像，而不是原始文本来编辑。
  • 检查记录结构与校验，编辑后重新计算对应格式。
  • 计划支持填充、地址偏移、裁剪、合并、转换与 CRC；尚未全部交付。

Ingot 仍处早期原型，功能以仓库中的实际代码为准。没有收费版、购买入口或试用门禁。

Understand and edit firmware images

A local workbench for Intel HEX, Motorola S-record and BIN files: inspect records, edit memory images and recalculate checksums safely.

Source and development statusBack to product catalog
Prototype stage · no verified production installer

What this product aims to solve

  • Work with address-mapped images rather than raw ASCII records.
  • Inspect record structures and checksums, recalculating on writes.
  • Fill, offset, crop, merge, conversion and CRC are planned; not all are delivered.

Ingot is an early prototype. The repository is the source of truth; there is no paid tier, checkout or license gate.
